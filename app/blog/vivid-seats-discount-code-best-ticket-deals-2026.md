What Is Vivid Seats?

Vivid Seats Rewards: Another Way to Save
A discount code isn't the only way to get more value from your ticket purchases. Vivid Seats offers a free loyalty program called Vivid Seats Rewards.
According to its published program information, members earn one stamp for each eligible ticket purchased. After collecting 10 stamps, members receive a Reward Credit based on the average eligible value of those tickets, excluding taxes, fees, and processing costs. Members can use Reward Credits toward eligible future purchases in the Vivid Seats app.
Here's how to get started:
Download the Vivid Seats app and sign in or create an account.
Join Vivid Seats Rewards when prompted.
Purchase eligible tickets and track your stamps.
Once you earn a Reward Credit, apply it to a future purchase through the app.
Check your account for credit balances, expiration dates, and applicable terms. If you regularly attend concerts or sporting events, rewards can become a useful part of your savings strategy.
5 Tips to Save More on Concert and Sports Tickets
A Vivid Seats discount code can help, but a few smart shopping habits can make a difference too.
1. Compare prices before booking
Check equivalent seats across ticket marketplaces. A lower listed price isn't always the cheapest final option once fees are included.
2. Look for offers directly from Vivid Seats
Sign up for promotional emails and check app notifications for event-specific or account-specific deals. Follow the offer's instructions to activate it correctly.
3. Shop early, but keep comparing
Booking early gives you more time to explore available seats. Prices and inventory can change, so compare options before committing rather than assuming the earliest listing is the best deal.
4. Check ticket details carefully
Review the seating location, event date, delivery method, and total amount due. Avoid choosing a ticket based only on its headline price.
5. Use rewards when eligible
If you have Vivid Seats Reward Credits, check whether you can apply them to your next app purchase. You can also look for an eligible discount code before completing checkout.
